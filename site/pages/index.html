For Mac, Windows and Linux · free and open source

Download the app, open it, and it appears in your menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows and Linux).

The app is not code-signed yet, so the first time you open it your computer warns you. How to open it anyway.

Open Terminal, paste this, press Return:

curl -fsSL https://{{domain}}/install | sh

On Windows, open PowerShell, paste this, press Enter:

irm https://{{domain}}/install.ps1 | iex

It downloads the app from GitHub and installs it, nothing else. What does this script do?

Using an AI assistant that can run commands, like Claude, ChatGPT or Codex? Paste this to it:

Please set up FreeAITokens on my computer by following https://{{domain}}/setup.md

It installs the app and connects your tools, and asks you to create the free accounts yourself. More about this.

Good to know

Free allowances are for trying things, not for running a business. Use your own accounts, one per provider, and don't share access with anyone. FreeAITokens runs on your own computer, and your keys are only ever sent to the provider they belong to. Some providers may use what you send to improve their models; the app tells you which.
