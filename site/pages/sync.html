What sync does

  • Your saved chats and provider keys appear on every computer where you turn on sync.
  • A change on one computer reaches the others within about a minute.
  • It works in the FreeAITokens app for Mac, Windows and Linux. The command-line tool doesn't sync.
  • Early access to new versions of the app: while a sync plan is active, the app offers new versions to you before it offers them to everyone.

What it doesn't do

  • The sync server can't read your data. Chats and keys are encrypted on your computer with a key only your passphrase opens. The server stores scrambled data, its size and when it changed.
  • Nobody can reset your passphrase. When you set up sync you get a recovery code. If you forget the passphrase and lose the recovery code, your synced data can't be opened. What's on your computers stays.
  • Pictures you create stay on the computer that made them. Their chats sync; the image files don't.
  • Sync doesn't give you AI access. You still use your own free accounts, one per provider.

How to start

  1. In the app, open Settings › Sync and choose a plan. The app picks up your key by itself.
  2. Choose a passphrase and save the recovery code it shows.
  3. On your other computers, choose "I already have a key" and enter the key and the passphrase.

If your plan ends

Sync stops, and nothing on your computers changes. Your encrypted data stays on the server for 90 days, so renewing picks up where you left off; after that it's deleted. A refunded or disputed payment stops sync at once, with the same 90 days.
