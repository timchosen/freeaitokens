About

AI tools are useful, and the subscriptions add up. Meanwhile dozens of AI companies give away a small free allowance each. FreeAITokens puts those allowances together, so one person can use AI tools for free.

How it works

FreeAITokens runs on your own computer. Your AI tools talk to it as if it were one AI service. For each request it picks the best free model that can handle it, and when that provider's free allowance runs out it moves to the next one. There is no FreeAITokens server in the middle: your requests go straight from your computer to the provider, with your own keys.

Who makes it

FreeAITokens is made by Timchosen Uzua, an independent developer, and is free and open source under the MIT licence. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or paid by any AI provider.

Staying up to date

Free offers change often. FreeAITokens checks a signed list of providers and models a few times a day, so new free models show up and retired ones disappear without you updating anything. The list is public in the source repository.
