8. Sync

Sync is an optional paid service: $10 a year or $49 once. Stripe sells it and takes the payment. A yearly plan renews until you cancel it in the app (Settings › Sync › Manage plan). If a payment is refunded or disputed, sync stops at once. When a plan ends or stops, nothing on your computers is touched, and your encrypted data is deleted from the server 90 days later. Keep your passphrase or recovery code: without them nobody, including us, can open your synced data.
