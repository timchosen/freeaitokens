Privacy

The short version: FreeAITokens doesn't collect anything about you. Optional paid sync stores only encrypted data it can't read.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking scripts.
  • It is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles the connection and keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.
  • Pages ask GitHub for the project's star count, so GitHub sees that request from your browser.

The app and the command

  • Your keys stay on your computer, in its keychain where it has one, and are only sent to the provider they belong to.
  • Your prompts and answers go straight between your computer and the AI provider. They never pass through a FreeAITokens server. What each provider does with them is set by its own privacy policy; providers that may use them for training are marked. If you turn on sync, your saved chats are also stored encrypted, as described below.
  • The provider list is downloaded a few times a day from a FreeAITokens service on Cloudflare. That request contains nothing about you or your use; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to Cloudflare.
  • No telemetry. The app doesn't report usage, errors or anything else.
  • The local address listens only on your own computer and needs a token, so other devices and websites can't use it.

Sync (optional, paid)

If you buy sync, a FreeAITokens server on Cloudflare stores:

  • Your chats and keys, encrypted on your computer before they're sent. The server never has your passphrase or the key that opens them, so it can't read them.
  • For each synced item: a random id, its size, a version number and when it changed.
  • A scrambled form (a hash) of your sync key, never the key itself.
  • A scrambled form (a keyed hash) of the email you paid with, only so the "send me my key" form can find your key. The email itself isn't kept.
  • Your plan, when it ends, and Stripe's ids for your payment and customer record.

Stripe sells sync and takes the payment, so your payment details and email go to Stripe under its privacy policy. When a plan ends, or a payment is refunded or disputed, your encrypted data is deleted 90 days later.
