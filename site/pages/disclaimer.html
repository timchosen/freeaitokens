You are using each provider's free tier

FreeAITokens does not give you AI. It helps you use the free allowances that each AI provider offers to its own users. Every request goes from your computer to that provider, under your account, and is governed by that provider's terms. You are responsible for following them. In particular:

  • Use your own accounts, one per provider. Don't create extra accounts to get more free use.
  • Don't share access. Never share your keys or your local FreeAITokens address with anyone, and never resell access.
  • Don't run a business on it. Free tiers exist for trying things and personal use, not production services.
  • Some providers have extra conditions, for example limiting use to professional purposes or to prototyping. The app shows these before you sign up.

What providers may do with your prompts

Some free tiers let the provider use what you send to improve their models, or are run by volunteers who can see it. FreeAITokens marks these providers in the app and in the provider list. Don't send anything private to a provider you're not comfortable with; you can remove any provider at any time.
